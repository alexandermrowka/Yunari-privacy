Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

1. General Information

Your privacy is important to us. Yunari is a dream journal and habit tracking app designed to let you record dreams using your voice and keep track of personal habits related to sleep and dreaming.

Yunari does not require you to create an account and does not collect or transmit your personal data to us.

2. Dream Entries and Habit Data

Information you enter into Yunari, including dream entries, voice recordings, transcriptions, notes, and habit tracking data, is stored locally on your device. This information is not transmitted to us and is not shared with third parties.

3. Microphone Access

Yunari may request access to your device's microphone so that you can record dream entries using your voice. Microphone access is only used when you choose to make a recording within the app.

Your audio recordings are not sent to us or used for advertising, analytics, or tracking purposes.

4. No Analytics, Advertising, or Tracking

Yunari does not use advertising services, analytics tools, cookies, or cross-app tracking technologies. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Third-Party Services

Yunari does not use third-party services or SDKs that collect personal data on our behalf.

6. Data Control and Deletion

Because your Yunari data is stored locally on your device, you remain in control of it. You can delete individual entries within the app or remove locally stored app data by deleting the app from your device.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if Yunari's features or data practices change. Any updates will be published on this page together with a revised "Last updated" date.
